## The one-line pitch

**Pseudo is an AI familiar for the Game Master — you summon it with a keystroke, and it answers your
questions, points at the exact button to click, reads your whole world, finds and opens your rules, and
even rolls dice and runs macros for you.**

## The name (a joke that happens to be true)

A **pseudodragon** is the classic fantasy familiar — a small, clever dragon bonded to a spellcaster. And
**"pseudo-"** means *artificial*. So Pseudo is, quite literally, a **pseudo-intelligence that behaves like
a familiar**: a made mind that perches on your shoulder and knows your world. The mascot is a
pseudodragon that breathes little puffs of smoke while it thinks.

## Who it's for

Foundry Virtual Tabletop **Game Masters** (and their players) — anyone who has ever paused a session to
dig through menus, hunt for a rule, or ask "wait, how do I do that again?"

## The problem it kills

Running a virtual tabletop means *tab-hunting*. Where's that setting? Which compendium has the grappling
rules? How do I add a player? What module does weather? Every one of those is a five-second detour that
breaks the table's momentum. **Pseudo collapses all of it into one keystroke and a plain-English
question.**

---

## The feature tour

### 1. Summon it anywhere — one keystroke

Press **Alt+D** and Pseudo's window appears. Press it again and it's gone. Ask anything in plain
language. The empty box even suggests things to try, rotating through prompts like *"Name five dive bars
in a cursed harbor town"* or *"How do I create an NPC?"* The window floats above your sheets, remembers
where you left it, and stays out of your way.

**Talk to it, literally.** A microphone button lets you **dictate your question by voice** — with a soft
"listening" chime — so you never even stop to type.

### 2. It answers *how-to* questions — and shows you where

Ask *"How do I manage modules?"* and Pseudo doesn't just describe it — it **spotlights the actual button
on your screen**, auto-switching to the right sidebar tab and even expanding a collapsed sidebar so you
can't miss it. A **"Show me where"** button replays the highlight any time.

Every answer is grounded in a real knowledge pack (core Foundry, your game system, your modules), so the
steps are correct — and if Pseudo isn't sure, it *says so* instead of inventing a menu that doesn't exist.

### 3. It reads YOUR world

Pseudo isn't answering from generic knowledge — it can **look at your actual game.** Ask *"How many
monsters do I have?"* and it counts them across every compendium and breaks it down by source:

> *"You have 865 monster (NPC) actors installed — Monsters (SRD): 331, Player's Handbook: 385…"*

Counts, lists, inventories of your spells, items, journals, scenes, roll tables — Pseudo reads them on
demand.

### 4. It finds your rules — and opens the page

Ask *"Find me the grappling rules"* and Pseudo searches your compendiums and journals, **summarizes the
rule, and opens the exact page** on your screen. Ask for a monster and it brings up the stat block. When
several things match, it hands you a tidy **pick-list** so you choose the right one.

### 5. It knows the whole web — without making things up

For anything your world doesn't cover — *"What module adds animated weather?"*, *"Is there a video
tutorial for dynamic lighting?"* — Pseudo does a **real web search** and answers with **genuine links**,
never invented ones. It even **embeds the YouTube tutorial right in the chat**, verified to actually
exist. It prefers trusted creators, and when it's truly stumped, it points you to a friendly community
Discord for help.

### 6. It recommends the right tools

Pseudo carries a curated shortlist of great modules. Ask about tracking in-game dates and it recommends
**Calendaria** with a real link; ask about wrangling your module list and it points you to **Big Bad
Module Manager**; ask for general help and it links the official Foundry tutorials — instantly, with no
fumbling for URLs.

### 7. It runs the table

Pseudo has hands. It can:

- 🎲 **Roll dice** — *"Roll 1d20+5"* rolls it straight to chat.
- 🎯 **Draw a roll table** — pulls a result from any table you've built.
- ⚙️ **Run a macro** — fires one of your existing macros.

…all safely. Macros are **GM-only** and always **ask for a confirming click first** — Pseudo can only
ever trigger things *you* already made, never invent code to run.

### 8. Set up in two minutes — and it's basically free

A friendly first-run **wizard** walks you through getting a **free** Google AI key. Paste it, click
**Test & Save**, and you're done. The key lives **only in your browser** — your players never see it —
and Pseudo runs on a fast, low-cost model, so a whole campaign's worth of questions costs pennies.

### 9. The whole table can use it

Set it up once as the GM, and **every player at the table can summon Pseudo too** — their questions are
answered through your key automatically. No GM online? Players get a gentle "check back when your GM is
here" instead of a broken feature.

### 10. It fixes and diagnoses itself

Pseudo tunes your search index for the best results and quietly hides duplicate, out-of-date content so
searches stay clean. And it ships with a **Troubleshooter**: one click generates a full diagnostic
report you can copy, export, or hand to the community — and Pseudo will even **diagnose its own setup**,
flagging problems in plain English.

### 11. Built to be trusted

Every powerful feature has a guardrail:

- It **never trusts a link the AI typed** — web links come only from real search results, videos are
  verified before they embed.
- It **never runs code the AI wrote** — it only triggers macros you created, and only after you confirm.
- Your **API key never leaves your browser** and never reaches your players.
- Answers you repeat are **cached**, so common questions are instant and cost nothing.
